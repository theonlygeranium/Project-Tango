# Session continuity

## Outcome

One paragraph stating the user-visible or operational outcome.

## What changed

- Durable facts only.
- Name the affected product, architecture, delivery, or operations surface.

## Evidence

| State | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Implemented | Exact files, PR, or commit |
| CI-verified | Exact workflow/check and revision |
| Deployed | Environment and deployment artifact |
| Live-verified | URL, timestamp, and observed behavior |

Omit rows without evidence; never promote an unverified state.

## Decisions

- Decision, rationale, and durable consequence.

## Risks and open gates

- Owner, next action, and what would prove closure.

## Wiki routing

- Canonical page to update → facts that belong there.
- Update log → one dated summary.
